Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most. Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out — see the Resources page.

Getting Started

Who is this site for?

Current students in our learning community. It's where you can read about how we teach, find helpful resources, and log in to your own portal.

How do I apply to Zenith, and what courses are available?

We don't have a formal application process set up yet — just contact us with any questions about applying or available courses, and we'll be glad to help.

Do I need an account to use the site?

No — the Home, Philosophy, Resources, and this FAQ page are open to everyone. Only the Portal requires logging in, since it shows your own portal page and calendar.

Finding Your Way

I don't know where to even start. What's the first thing I should do?

Reach out to us first. Once you're enrolled, we'll set up your Portal with a course roadmap, and the Now page will always tell you the single next thing to work on — you don't have to figure that part out yourself.

What grade should I start thinking about all this?

Earlier is easier, but there's no hard cutoff. We work with students across grade levels — your plan is built around how much runway you actually have left, not a fixed grade-by-grade script.

Is it too late if I haven't started yet?

No. We reject the idea that where you're starting from decides where you can end up — see our philosophy. What matters is starting now and grinding consistently from here.

What should I actually be doing in 8th/9th vs 10th vs 11th vs 12th grade?

This depends heavily on your specific courses and goals, which is exactly why we build individual roadmaps instead of a one-size-fits-all checklist. Reach out and we'll help you figure out where you stand and what comes next.

I don't know what I'm interested in yet — is that a problem?

Not at all. Most students don't have it figured out this early either. Our job is to keep the actual work — curriculum, testing, feedback — clear and moving, so you have room to figure out your interests along the way instead of stalling until you do.

I feel behind compared to other students. What do I do?

Comparing yourself to others is the fastest way to lose motivation without gaining anything. We care about your own trajectory — where you started versus where you are now, not where you rank against classmates. Talk to us and we'll build a realistic plan from wherever you're at.

What's the biggest mistake students make early on?

Passively consuming information — rewatching lectures, rereading notes — without being tested on it directly. Real growth comes from active effort: doing the work, getting checked, and fixing what's wrong. That's why regular testing and direct feedback are central to how we operate.

Do I need to have everything figured out before I start?

No — figuring things out along the way is normal. What we need from you is consistent effort once you're in the system; the direction is on us.

Using the Site

What's in my Portal?

Once you're logged in, your Portal shows your unlocked units and due tasks, several mock tests to help keep you on track, and a calendar with your session schedule.

What if my Portal or Calendar doesn't load?

Just contact us and we'll help sort it out.

Is the Resources page only for logged-in students?

No — it's open to anyone, no login required.

What does the ★ next to a resource mean?

It marks something we personally recommend highly. Not every resource gets one, so treat it as a strong signal, not just part of the listing.

Your Course & Roadmap

I'm enrolled in more than one course — how do I switch between them?

Log in and go to Courses (the Portal's first tab) — you'll see a folder of every course you're enrolled in. Pick one to open its roadmap; "All courses" in the top bar always brings you back to that list.

What do the roadmap categories (Book, Coursework, Solution Manual, etc.) mean?

Each roadmap chapter is broken into steps — Book Chapter, Coursework, Solution Manual, Review, Test, and Mock, plus Information and Notes Submission for intro material — so you always know what kind of work an item actually is, not just its name.

What do the statuses (Locked, Unlocked, Review, Complete) mean?

Unlocked (marked with a ★) is the thing to work on right now. Locked means it isn't relevant yet — it recedes so it doesn't compete for your attention. Review and Optional Reading are extra material worth a look, and Complete means you're done with it.

What's the difference between Table view and Curve view?

Table view lists every item in a chapter with its category, status, and links — best when you want the full detail. Curve view plots your whole course as a single journey, chapter by chapter, so you can see your overall progress at a glance; tap a point for the details behind it.

What's the Now page for?

It cuts through the full roadmap and shows you one thing: the single next task to work on, with instructions and a due date if there is one. When you're not sure what to do next, start there.

What's on the Feedback page, and what's the Cheat Sheet?

Feedback collects the notes we leave you as you work through the roadmap. Anything worth remembering gets pulled into your Cheat Sheet automatically, so your past mistakes turn into a single reference instead of getting lost in old feedback.

What's the Submit page?

It's where you turn in work tied to your course — the submission form loads right there in the Portal so you don't need to go hunting for a separate link.

What's the Calendar page for?

It shows your session schedule — Zoom or IRL — so you know what's coming up without having to ask.
